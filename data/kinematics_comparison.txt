================================================================================================
3. SYNTHESIS OF SCIENTIFIC TAKEAWAYS
================================================================================================
1. Statistical Hierarchy & Physical Realism:
   - Ballistic models (Models 1 & 2) assume constant expansion across all epochs.
   - Piecewise deceleration models (Models 3 & 4) capture the observed sharp break
     around MJD ~60805-60820, dropping from relativistic launch speeds to ~5-7 mas/day.
2. Superluminal Apparent Motion:
   - Unconstrained distance propagation (D = 9.4 ± 1.0 kpc) yields apparent expansion
     beta_app > 1.0c for the initial approaching lobe in piecewise models.
3. Impact of Observational Upper Limits:
   - Non-detections in early blocks (MJDs 60736 and 60758) penalize models that
     predict early knot separation before observed resolution.
